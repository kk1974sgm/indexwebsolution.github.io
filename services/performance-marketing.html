What Is Performance Marketing?

Performance marketing is a digital marketing strategy where every campaign is measured against specific business objectives such as lead generation, online sales, appointment bookings, or customer acquisitions. Unlike traditional advertising, performance marketing focuses on measurable metrics including:

  • Website traffic
  • Qualified leads
  • Conversion rates
  • Cost Per Lead (CPL)
  • Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)
  • Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
  • Revenue generated
  • Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Every marketing decision is based on data, allowing businesses to optimize campaigns and continuously improve performance.
